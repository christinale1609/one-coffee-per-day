# Interview guide

The goal is to learn about **pain points and the job to be done**, not to pitch. Follow the Mom Test: ask about specifics in the past, talk less, listen more. Show the app **only at the end**, if at all.

Aim for 30 minutes. Interview one person at a time. Record (with permission) or use an AI notetaker, then fill in the findings template below right after.

---

## Intro (1 min)

> "Thanks for making time. We're students working on a project about how people network for jobs. There are no right answers. We just want to hear about your real experience. Is it OK if I take notes?"

## Background (3 min)

- What are you studying or working on right now?
- What kind of role are you hoping to land next? In which industry or companies?
- Where are you in your search right now?

## Understanding the job (10 min): ask about the past, not the future

- Tell me about the **last time** you reached out to someone you didn't know for career advice. Walk me through it from the start.
  - How did you decide who to contact?
  - How did you find them? How long did that take?
  - What did you write? How long did it take to write?
  - What happened next?
- How many people have you reached out to in the last month? How many replied?
- After you got a coffee chat, how did you prepare? What did you ask?
- How do you keep track of who you've talked to and when to follow up?

## Pains and workarounds (8 min)

- What's the hardest part of networking for you? Why?
- Tell me about a time it went badly, or felt awkward.
- What tools or tricks do you use today? (Spreadsheets, LinkedIn Premium, ChatGPT, templates, career centre…) What do you like or dislike about them?
- Have you paid for anything to help with this? (LinkedIn Premium, coaching…)

### Rank these from 1 (not important) to 5 (must have)

- Knowing *who* to reach out to
- Help writing the connection note
- Staying under LinkedIn's character limit
- Tracking who I've contacted and when to follow up
- Research on the person before a chat
- Suggested questions to ask
- Reminders to follow up or say thanks

## (Optional) Show the product (5 min)

Only after the questions above. Demo the happy path with realistic data: resume → people → note → tracker → prep brief.

- What's your first reaction?
- What would you use? What could you live without?
- What's missing or confusing?

## The value question: ask for skin in the game (2 min)

People are polite, so "I like it" means nothing. Ask for something real:

- **Time:** "Would you try it this week and set up one coffee chat with it? Can I check in with you on Friday?"
- **Intros:** "Who else do you know who's networking right now who'd be good to talk to?"
- **Money:** "If this cost $5 a month, would you pay for it? What would make it worth it?"
- **NPS:** "On a scale of 0-10, how likely are you to recommend it to a friend? Why that number?"

## Synthesis (after 2+ interviews)

- **Patterns:** what came up in more than one interview?
- **Hypotheses check:** which of H1-H5 in `product-brief.md` look supported or contradicted?
- **Decisions:** what will we build, change or cut?
